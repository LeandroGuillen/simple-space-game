## 3.3.2.2 Population Growth
The 'base growth rate' is ( (number of colonists)*(space left on planet) *1000)
^0.5 (I _think_. Basically this ranges from ~40 to ~80). Then this number is
modified by things like race growth rate, starvation, etc. Finally, +100 is
added for a cloning center. Multi-species colonies have different growth rates
for each species. Starvation causes -50 growth per missing food.
